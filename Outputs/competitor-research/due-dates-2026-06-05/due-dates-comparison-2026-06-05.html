Competitor comparison: Due dates & scheduling

Date: 2026-06-05 · Competitors: todoist

Scope

How Todoist assigns, reschedules, and surfaces due dates — NLP capture, recurrence, duration, deadlines (Pro), and Today/Upcoming views. Single-competitor run; no logged-in UI capture on this environment.

Per-competitor notes

todoist

  • Dual model: scheduled date vs hard deadline
  • Quick Add is the primary capture surface for dates and recurrence
  • Recurring completion semantics can shift next due if completed after midnight
  • Duration requires time; 24h cap; no multi-day duration span
  • Deep scheduling controls in task view right sidebar on web/desktop
